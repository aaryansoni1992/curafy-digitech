Healthcare & Clinic Practice

Patient Appointment Booking & Local Discovery Funnel

Multi-Channel Acquisition
The Challenge

Medical practices often face empty doctor OPD slots due to fragmented online presence, lack of Google Business Maps visibility, and complicated booking processes that deter patients.

The Strategy

Curafy Digitech implemented high-intent Google Search ads focused on specialist treatments, paired with 1-click WhatsApp appointment confirmations and patient review authority on Google Maps.

The Outcome

Established predictable daily patient appointment enquiries, reduced friction in scheduling consultations, and built durable local search ranking for specialist treatments.
