# Curafy Digitech - Digital Growth & Performance Lab

Curafy Digitech is a premier, futuristic high-contrast digital marketing & performance agency web platform engineered for Hospitals & Clinics, Local Businesses, Startups, and Corporate Enterprises.

## ✨ Features

- **Futuristic 3D & Neon Design:** Option 1 Deep Royal Blue (`#0A0F1D`) with Electric Cyan (`#00F0FF`) & Emerald accents, featuring interactive glassmorphism, 3D tilt mechanics, and micro-interactions.
- **Dedicated Sector Portal:** 14 target sectors with interactive category filtering (Hospitality, Real Estate, Education, Food & Restaurants, Automobile, Beauty & Wellness, Fashion, Jewellery, Professional Services, Manufacturing, E-commerce, Travel & Tourism, Events & Weddings, Healthcare).
- **Core 05 Services Architecture:**
  1. Digital Marketing (Strategy, Brand Promotion, Campaign Planning)
  2. Social Media Marketing (Instagram, Facebook, Reels, Engagement)
  3. Performance Marketing (Meta & Google Ads, Lead Gen, ROAS Tracking)
  4. SEO & Local SEO (Keyword Research, Technical SEO, GBP Optimization)
  5. Website & Landing Pages (High-Conversion UI/UX, Speed Optimized)
- **Direct Engagement & WhatsApp Integration:**
  - One-click WhatsApp Call button (`6376566383`)
  - Direct WhatsApp enquiry modal and audit request forms
- **Social Proof & Brand Marquee:**
  - Continuous dual-lane marquee showcasing "Little & Big" company partnerships
  - Enterprise outcomes & startup metrics
- **Multi-Page Experience:**
  - `index.html` - Main command center & hero showcase
  - `services.html` - In-depth breakdown of all 5 service pillars
  - `healthcare.html` - Specialized medical & clinic marketing solutions
  - `case-studies.html` - Little vs. Big verified ROI case studies
  - `about.html` - Agency vision, values & leadership
  - `contact.html` - Interactive enquiry form & scheduling

## 🚀 Local Development

To run the local server on Windows:
```powershell
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\serve.ps1 -Port 5500
```
Then visit: `http://localhost:5500`
