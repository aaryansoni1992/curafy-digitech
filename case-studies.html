Big Enterprise • Healthcare Network

Apollo & Metro Superspecialty Hospital: +340% Patient Bookings in 90 Days

Overall ROAS 6.4x
The Challenge

The hospital had a modern facility and renowned doctors, but their OPD chairs were frequently empty. Their previous ad agency was generating unqualified clicks with no direct appointments.

The Strategy

Curafy Digitech implemented high-intent Google Search ads focused on surgery and specialist keywords, combined with a 1-click streamlined OPD booking calendar and doctor trust-building video testimonials.

The Results

OPD bookings grew from 74/month to 325+/month. Cost per confirmed patient dropped from ₹680 to ₹210, generating over ₹52 Lakh in surgery revenue.

Little Boutique • Luxury Retail

Royal Heritage Jewellery Studio: ₹74 Lakh Festive Sales from Hyper-Local Ads

Footfall Increase +420%
The Challenge

The brand opened an expansive bridal showroom but faced stiff competition from legacy players in the local market. They needed verified bridal shoppers to walk into the store.

The Strategy

Curafy built an exclusive VIP Bridal Consultation invitation funnel on Meta Ads (Instagram Reels) geofenced to wealthy zip codes within a 12km radius.

The Results

Generated 240+ private bridal showroom appointments in 45 days, converting into ₹74 Lakh in direct jewellery sales at 5.8x ROAS.
